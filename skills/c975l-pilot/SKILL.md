# c975L PilotBundle

> Spaces of cards piloted on their own pages, each card able to run an action on a runner machine and come back for validation, shared between the members of a space.

**Package:** `c975l/pilot-bundle` · **Namespace:** `c975L\PilotBundle\` · **Tables:** `pilot_*`

**Key source paths** (relative to the package root):
`src/Entity/`, `src/Enum/`, `src/Repository/`, `src/Security/`, `src/Service/`, `src/Form/`, `src/Command/`, `src/Controller/`, `src/Mcp/`, `src/Management/`, `src/Twig/`, `templates/pilot/`, `assets/`, `config/services.yaml`, `config/configs.json`

**Related skills:** `c975l-config` (the `pilot-horizon-days` key), `c975l-management` (the back office's link to the piloting pages)

**Related documentation:** this package's `README.md` is the reference. The ecosystem's rules live in `c975l/core-bundle`.

## The model

- A `Project` is a space (« Espace » on screen): its cards, files, plans, members and collections. A `CardSet` (« Collection » on screen, `CardSetController` on the space's page, the `collection` kind of the settings) gathers cards of one kind with the word for one of them (`getItem()`) and the checklist template a card added to it gets (`getChecklistTemplate()`, through `CardFiller`); `Card::setCardSet()` keeps a card to collections of its own space. A day is coloured by its nearness by `templates/pilot/_due.html.twig`. A card carries a source and a result link (`getSourceUrl()`, `getResultUrl()`, http(s) only), edited in its dialog and written `- Source:` / `- Result:` in its markdown. Its `slug` opens `/pilot/{slug}` and never takes one of the pages' own words (`Project::SLUG`).
- A `Card` without a date is in the backlog (`isInBacklog()`), with one it shows on the dashboard; `isDone()` tells the done ones (a tile on a grey background). In a `Plan` it is a step at a position, done by a human or a runner (`Executor`), as a task or a validation (`CardKind`). Its template is its plan's or the checklist one it was laid from (`Card::getTemplate()`, `follow()`). Its checklist holds its steps then its feedback (`getSteps()`, `getFeedback()`, `addFeedback()`), and a done card opens again once a box is to tick (`toggleStep()`, `addStep()`, `restep()`). It is given to members (`addAssignee()`, `isAssignedTo()`) and moved to another space (`moveTo()`, the `move` gesture, refused for a card of a plan). A step may carry a group `g`, laid from its template step's `group` (`StepOrder::grouped()`), `getStepGroups()` giving the runs of one group the card folds.
- A `Member` gives an account (ConfigBundle's `UserInterface`) a `MemberRole` in a space: guest (the cards given to it), contributor (every card), editor (the machines, deleting a card), owner (the space's settings and members). `PilotAccess::can()` answers per level (`VIEW`, `WORK`, `EDIT`, `RUN`, `DELETE`, `MANAGE`), the site's admin holding them all, and `scope()` gives the `PilotScope` every list query takes (`restrictCards()`, `restrictProjects()`).
- A `Doc` has two floors: `getSummary()` (five lines a human reads) and `getDetail()` (folded, for Claude). `write()` sets both at once.
- A `Run` is one execution of a runner card. A `Machine` claims it under a lease of `Run::LEASE_SECONDS`, reports progress, then finishes it.

### Guided projects

`Management\PilotGuidedProjectProvider` gives ConfigBundle's guided projects its parcours in the 12000 block: `pilot-space`, `pilot-machine`, `pilot-template` and `pilot-credits` (the settings' forms, for `site-role-admin`), `pilot-card`, `pilot-steps`, `pilot-number`, `pilot-run` and `pilot-agent` (a step handed to an AI agent working through the MCP endpoint) (from the dashboard to a space, a card and its gestures, for whoever opens the projects' page). They walk the piloting pages, which carry the panel themselves: `_layout.html.twig` loads `@c975l/config-bundle/controllers-admin.js` beside the bundle's script, draws the panel's few rules in its nonced `<style>`, and ends on the Twig function `pilot_guided_project_mount()` (`PilotGuidedProjectExtension`, ConfigBundle's `GuidedProjectMountBuilder`). ## The runner

A runner is any program, on a workstation or a server, polling `/api/pilot/runner/claim` with its machine's token, in any language able to send HTTP and read JSON; the site never sends a command, only an action key and its parameters. The key is the link: each call declares the keys the machine runs (`Action` rows), a card or a checklist step names one (`Card::setStepAction()`, picked in the step's form, the `stepedit` gesture), and `RunRepository::queuedIds()` gives a run only to a machine that declared it, among the spaces it serves (`Machine::serves()`, `projectIds()`, every space when none is chosen); `CardFlow` queues a run only for an action offered to the card's space (`expectOffered()`, `OfferedToProjectsTrait::isOfferedTo()`), whoever names it. A machine writes a report only on a space it serves (`ReportIngester::ingest()`), and a file only in its card's space (`DocIngester`). The reference runner ships in `runner/` (`pilot-runner.py`, with `runner.yaml.dist` listing the sites served and `actions.yaml.dist`, read from `$PILOT_RUNNER_CONFIG` or `~/.config/pilot-runner`; `--once` for one round, `--install` for a systemd user service, the card's `title`, `note`, `due` (its date, `YYYY-MM-DD`), `project`, `doc` and `requester` (`Run::getRequester()`, set by `CardFlow` from `CardFlow::displayName()` of the user who queues it, empty when nobody signed in asked, a scheduled task for instance) given as `{placeholders}` and the site's URL to each command as `PILOT_URL`, the last line a failed command prints shown as its reason, a `TITLE <text>` line renaming the card through the `title` of `runs/{id}/finish`, a `RESULT <url>` line setting its result address (`Card::setResultUrl()`, an address that is no http(s) one ignored) through its `result`, a `FEEDBACK <n> <reason>` line leaving open the feedback numbered n of the comment it was handed, sent as `feedback: {n: reason}`, a `SPENT <credit> <amount>` line telling what the run spent, sent as `spent: {key: amount}`, a `LINK <url> [words]` line leaving a link under its step, sent as `links: [{url, label}]` and kept by `Run::setLinks()` (http(s) only, `Run::MAX_LINKS`, named by its host without words), shown under the step once its run is done) and is generic: its `actions.yaml` maps each key to a command (`label`, `help`, `argv` with `{param}` placeholders, `params` with the pattern each value must match, `outputs`), so an action is a script declared there. `Machine::getParallel()` (the « Runs in parallel » setting, 1 to `Machine::MAX_PARALLEL`) is sent in the `X-Pilot-Parallel` header of every claim answer; the runner keeps that many runs in threads, an action marked `exclusive: true` in `actions.yaml` started only on an idle machine and holding it alone, the claim's `only` list (`RunQueue::claim()`'s last argument) narrowing the queue to the actions startable now. `Run::getStartedAt()` (set by `claim()`, the queue time for older runs) and `RunRepository::typicalSeconds()` (median of the action's last runs done) feed the Twig function `pilot_run_progress(run)` (`PilotRunExtension`), the progress shown: the reported one, or an estimate up to `ESTIMATE_MAX` (95) flagged `estimated` and written « ≈ », also in the `pilot-run-watch` state so the card is read again as it moves; the list of runs under way of the dashboard and of a space (`_runs.html.twig`) carries its own `pilot-run-watch` with a `runs` value (the ids listed), read again every 10 s, the list swapped in as the runs move and the page reloaded once one ends or starts, skipped while a card is open or a field edited. A `FILE /path` line of a command reaches `finish` as `files` (`RunQueue::finish()`'s last argument, `Run::setFiles()`: absolute paths, once each, `Run::MAX_FILES`), shown under the step by `Run::getFilesByFolder()` once its run is done (the machine's own data, `Run::HIDDEN_FILES` such as a `.json` or a `.log`, kept on the run but left out, with a folder holding nothing else; each folder by its name, its path in the `title`, then its files one by one with an icon by extension), never uploaded, and greyed with a « To redo » badge when `Card::staleSteps()` lists the step (an earlier step's done run wrote files after its own); a ticked step stores `doneAt` to the minute and `doneBy` (`Card::toggleStep()`'s last argument: `CardFlow::initials()` of the user who ticks, the machine's name when its run ticks it), shown after the step's text with the run's date when it made files; the Twig function `pilot_open_url(path)` (`PilotUrlExtension::open()`, config `pilot-open-scheme`, a web scheme refused) makes each a `<scheme>:///path` link, served on each computer by `runner/pilot-open.py` (`--install`, the folders allowed under `open:` in its `runner.yaml`, a program never opened).

```bash
php bin/console c975l:pilot:machine-add <name>
```

prints a machine's token once, only its sha256 being kept (`Machine::newToken()`, `MachineRepository::findOneByToken()`). The machine calls `RunnerController` under `/api/pilot/runner` with `Authorization: Bearer <token>`: `claim` (the actions it declares, recorded as `Action` rows by `ActionRepository::declare()`, only the machine that declared an action first, `Action::getMachine()`, rewording it afterwards), `runs/{id}/progress`, `runs/{id}/output`, and `runs/{id}/finish`. `RunQueue` takes a run in one conditional `UPDATE` (`RunRepository::take()`), refuses news about a run the machine no longer holds (409) and puts back the runs whose lease ended. `RunOutputs` keeps the files under `private/pilot/runs/<run id>/`, each name reduced to a safe one, `RunOutputs::fits()` holding a run to `MAX_FILES` files and `MAX_RUN_BYTES` (413 beyond), and `PilotController::output()` shows an image or a video under the type of its extension (`RunOutputs::mediaType()`) and downloads any other file, sandboxed.

The runner API's lock: `RunnerController::machine()` refuses an address past `RunnerController::DENIED_LIMITER` (20 unknown tokens per hour, prepended by `c975LPilotBundle::prependExtension()`) before looking its token up, and `denied()` counts every unknown token, answering 429 past the ceiling. The reference runner's `secure()` leaves aside a site in plain `http://` off `127.0.0.1`, `localhost` or `::1`. A stolen token reaches only what its machine serves: `RunQueue::claim()` within `Machine::projectIds()`, `RunQueue::expectHeld()` on every report, `ReportIngester` and `DocIngester` within its spaces, and `RunQueue::stepSpent()`, which keeps of `spent` only the credit the step's cost names. A file named by a run is shown under its step and opened only through `pilot_open_url()`, never as a `vscode://` or `file://` link. The README's « Security of the runner API » is the reference for site owners: keep it true when any of these changes.

## The MCP server

`PilotMcpProvider` (`McpToolProviderInterface` of c975l/core-bundle, key `pilot`) hands the site's MCP endpoint `/api/mcp` the methods of `PilotTools` and `PilotDocTools` (the `AccountTools` trait keeping both to the agent token's account through `PilotAccess`) as tools: pilot_spaces (with the names of each space's collections), pilot_cards (each with its collection), pilot_card (the `CardMarkdown::write()` text, the card's absolute address from `PilotUrls::shared()` (`/pilot/{space}#card-{id}`, its space's page opening it in its dialog) and the index of each step and comment, a step's action and its last run from `Card::stepRuns()`), pilot_add_card (through `CardFiller::card()`), pilot_tick_step (a step or a comment by its checklist index, through `CardFlow::tick()` as the `step` gesture) pilot_comment (`Card::addFeedback()`), pilot_edit_card (the fields given only, a collection found by its name, a file of its space by its slug, a card of a plan kept in its space), pilot_add_steps (`Card::addStep()`), pilot_close_card (`CardFlow::complete()`, or `Card::markDone(null)`), pilot_run_step (`CardFlow::runStep()`), pilot_cancel_run (`CardFlow::cancel()`, the run given or the last, as the `cancel` gesture), pilot_validate_run (`CardFlow::validate()`, as the `validate` gesture), pilot_merge_cards (`Card::absorb()`, then the merged card removed) pilot_delete_card, and for the files pilot_docs (`DocRepository::search()` or `findFiles()`), pilot_doc (`Doc::toMarkdown()`) and pilot_write_doc (`DocRepository::newFile()`, `Doc::write()`), every file opened at `EDIT` as its page guards it (a guest reading none, refused as unknown, but the file attached to a card given to it read with that card in pilot_card), a file of no space reached only by the admin. A card is deleted, from its page as from the MCP, by `CardRemoval::remove()`, refused while a machine works on it, its files removed once the removal is flushed. Everything is kept to the account: `PilotAccess::scope()` for the lists, `PilotAccess::can()` at each tool's level (`WORK` to tick or comment, `EDIT` to change, `RUN` to run a step, cancel a run or validate its result, `DELETE` to merge away or delete), a space or card it does not see answering as unknown and a level its role does not hold said so. A refusal is a `ToolCallException`, shown to the agent as a tool error. A `FlowException` comes back in the site's words. The reading tools carry `readOnlyHint`, the writing ones not, merging and deleting `destructiveHint`, the confirmation being left to the client.

## Importing the artifact

```bash
php bin/console c975l:pilot:import <export-folder>
```

The folder holds one sub-folder per collection of the artifact database (`settings`, `tasks`, `docs`), one JSON file per document, the character sheets left aside. `ArtifactReader` reads it into rows, `ArtifactImporter` writes them. A second import updates instead of duplicating: projects and files are matched on their slug, cards on `project:id`, each matched card getting back the artifact's title, note, date, checklist and done state.

## Do not

- Re-import once cards are dated or closed in the back office: the import puts the artifact's values back on every card it matches.
- Put a project colour in a `style` attribute: the Content-Security-Policy refuses it, the colour goes through a `pilot-color-<slug>` class written in the nonced `<style>` of `_layout.html.twig`.
- Reach a card or a space in a tool by a bare `find()`: every one goes through the machine's scope or `Machine::serves()`, or a token handed to an agent reads every space.
- Pass a machine token in a URL, or serve a run's file inline under a sniffed type: a machine can send an html or svg file, `PilotController::output()` downloads it.
- Check a right in a template only: every action goes through `PilotPageTrait::guard()` (404 for what the account does not see, 403 or a refusal for what its role does not hold), and every list through `PilotScope`.
- Point a setting at EasyAdmin: the settings live on `SettingsController`, the back office only links to `/pilot`.

A credit naming a service (`Credit::getProvider()`, none for a balance typed in on the dashboard, `isManual()`) is read from its API with its own key by `CreditRefresher::refresh()`, through the `CreditReaderInterface` answering that provider (`ElevenLabsReader`, `DIdReader`, `ExpoReader` for `expo-ios` and `expo-android` with an expo.dev access token and the account in `Credit::getAccount()`; an app adds a service by implementing the interface, autoconfigured under `CreditReaderInterface::TAG`). The reading sets the remaining, total and renewal (`Credit::read()`, a `CreditBalance`), the label, unit, budget and note staying the admin's; a key that no longer decrypts or a service that does not answer keeps the last balance and logs a warning. `CreditRefresher::refreshAll()` reads them all, every morning through `c975l:pilot:credits-refresh` and `Scheduler\PilotMaintenanceTaskProvider`, and from the dashboard's button (`PilotController::creditsRefresh()`, `pilot_credits_refresh`). A run's `SPENT <credit> <amount>` lines, sent by the runner as `spent: {key: amount}` in `runs/{id}/finish`, go through `RunQueue::finish()` to `CreditRefresher::spend()`: only the credit the cost of the run's step names (`RunQueue::stepSpent()`, nothing for a run without one), taken off a balance typed in by hand, never below zero, a service's balance left to the morning refresh and the dashboard's button, never read at a run's end.
