# c975L PilotBundle

> Spaces of cards piloted on their own pages, each card able to run an action on a runner machine and come back for validation, shared between the members of a space.

**Package:** `c975l/pilot-bundle` (private) · **Namespace:** `c975L\PilotBundle\` · **Tables:** `pilot_*`

**Key source paths** (relative to the package root):
`src/Entity/`, `src/Enum/`, `src/Repository/`, `src/Security/`, `src/Service/`, `src/Form/`, `src/Command/`, `src/Controller/`, `src/Management/`, `src/Twig/`, `templates/pilot/`, `templates/share/`, `assets/`, `config/services.yaml`, `config/configs.json`

**Related skills:** `c975l-config` (the `pilot-horizon-days` key), `c975l-management` (the back office's link to the piloting pages)

**Related documentation:** this package's `README.md` is the reference. The ecosystem's rules live in `c975l/core-bundle`.

## The model

- A `Project` is a space (« Espace » on screen): its cards, files, plans, members and collections. A `CardSet` (« Collection » on screen, `CardSetController` on the space's page, the `collection` kind of the settings) gathers cards of one kind with the word for one of them (`getItem()`) and the checklist template a card added to it gets (`getChecklistTemplate()`, through `CardFiller`); `Card::setCardSet()` keeps a card to collections of its own space. A step may carry the day it is planned for (`due`, `Card::setStepDue()`, the `stepdue` gesture, `@YYYY-MM-DD` after its text in the markdown), coloured like every day by `templates/pilot/_due.html.twig`. A card carries a source and a result link (`getSourceUrl()`, `getResultUrl()`, http(s) only), edited in its dialog and written `- Source:` / `- Result:` in its markdown. Its `slug` opens `/pilot/{slug}` and never takes one of the pages' own words (`Project::SLUG`).
- A `Card` without a date is in the backlog (`isInBacklog()`), with one it shows on the dashboard; `isDone()` tells the done ones (a tile on a grey background). In a `Plan` it is a step at a position, done by a human or a runner (`Executor`), as a task or a validation (`CardKind`). Its template is its plan's or the checklist one it was laid from (`Card::getTemplate()`, `follow()`). Its checklist holds its steps then its feedback (`getSteps()`, `getFeedback()`, `addFeedback()`), and a done card opens again once a box is to tick (`toggleStep()`, `addStep()`, `restep()`). It is given to members (`addAssignee()`, `isAssignedTo()`) and moved to another space (`moveTo()`, the `move` gesture, refused for a card of a plan). A step may carry a group `g`, laid from its template step's `group` (`StepOrder::grouped()`), `getStepGroups()` giving the runs of one group the card folds.
- A `Member` gives an account (ConfigBundle's `UserInterface`) a `MemberRole` in a space: guest (the cards given to it), contributor (every card), editor (the machines, lending the editing link, deleting a card), owner (the space's settings and members). `PilotAccess::can()` answers per level (`VIEW`, `WORK`, `EDIT`, `RUN`, `DELETE`, `MANAGE`), the site's admin holding them all, and `scope()` gives the `PilotScope` every list query takes (`restrictCards()`, `restrictProjects()`).
- A `Doc` has two floors: `getSummary()` (five lines a human reads) and `getDetail()` (folded, for Claude). `write()` sets both at once.
- A `Run` is one execution of a runner card. A `Machine` claims it under a lease of `Run::LEASE_SECONDS`, reports progress, then finishes it.

A file is deleted from its page by `PilotController::docDelete()` (`pilot_doc_delete`, `PilotAccess::DELETE` in its space, the admin for one of no space), the cards citing it left without one; its headings are kept to its text's size (`.pilot-doc-body`). `MemberController` lets a space's owner add an account by its email, change its role or take it out. `SettingsController` lists, creates, edits and deletes the spaces, plan templates (`PlanStepType` steps added and removed by `assets/js/pilot-collection.js`, a checklist template saved laying its steps through `StepOrder::fromTemplate()`), machine actions, machines (the spaces each serves, its token shown once) and credits, as `SettingsKinds` describes them; the back office only shows a link to the dashboard (`MenuProvider::getLinks()`).

`CardMarkdown` writes a card, its steps and feedback (`## Steps`, `## Feedback`, a screenshot as `  ![screenshot](<absolute address>)` on the line under its feedback, read back onto it) and its reference as one markdown text, and `read()` lays such a text back (a part left out keeping what the card had). A card's page renders each step through `_step.html.twig`, a ticked one left in its place without a drag handle, several ticked in a row folded where they stand in a closed `details` inside `li.pilot-steps-done` (`label.steps_ticked`), from the `parts` of `Card::getStepGroups()`. The card's page holds that text in a hidden `#pilot-ai-context-<id>` and gives each open step and feedback a robot button (`_ai_share.html.twig`, the `pilot-copy` controller with its `context` value) copying `label.ai_task` naming the step, then the markdown, to paste into a Claude session. Two secrets give the card outside the piloting pages, the link being the permission: `share` makes `Card::getShareToken()` (read-only), `lend` makes `Card::getEditToken()` (read, rewrite and act, so lending it asks `PilotAccess::RUN`); `ShareController` serves `/pilot/share/{token}`, its `.md`, a POST rewriting the card, and `/pilot/share/{token}/{step|stepup|feedback|runstep|cancel|entrust}` under the editing secret. A checklist step waits for the step before, or for the steps its `after` names by key (`Card::stepKey()`, `StepOrder::linkAfter()`); `Card::stepNeeds()`, `stepAwaits()` and `isStepWaiting()` give the state, a feedback waiting for nothing. `StepOrder` reorders only a card of its own: a card following a checklist template takes its order from the template alone (`StepOrder::reorder()` leaves it, `follow()` puts a moved markdown back), the template saved laying its steps on its open cards (`CardRepository::findOpenOfTemplate()`), a template step whose title holds a `{variable}` known on the cards that filled it.

## The runner

A runner is any program, on a workstation or a server, polling `/api/pilot/runner/claim` with its machine's token, in any language able to send HTTP and read JSON; the site never sends a command, only an action key and its parameters. The key is the link: each call declares the keys the machine runs (`Action` rows), a card or a checklist step names one (`Card::setStepAction()`, picked in the step's form, the `stepedit` gesture), and `RunRepository::queuedIds()` gives a run only to a machine that declared it, among the spaces it serves (`Machine::serves()`, `projectIds()`, every space when none is chosen). A machine writes a report only on a space it serves (`ReportIngester::ingest()`), a file only in its card's space (`DocIngester`), and reports the balances only when it serves every space (`Machine::servesAll()`). The reference runner ships in `runner/` (`pilot-runner.py`, with `runner.yaml.dist` listing the sites served and `actions.yaml.dist`, read from `$PILOT_RUNNER_CONFIG` or `~/.config/pilot-runner`; `--once` for one round, `--install` for a systemd user service, the site's URL given to each command as `PILOT_URL`, the last line a failed command prints shown as its reason, a `TITLE <text>` line renaming the card through the `title` of `runs/{id}/finish`) and is generic: its `actions.yaml` maps each key to a command (`label`, `help`, `argv` with `{param}` placeholders, `params` with the pattern each value must match, `outputs`), so an action is a script declared there.

```bash
php bin/console c975l:pilot:machine-add <name>
```

prints a machine's token once, only its sha256 being kept (`Machine::newToken()`, `MachineRepository::findOneByToken()`). The machine calls `RunnerController` under `/api/pilot/runner` with `Authorization: Bearer <token>`: `claim` (the actions it declares, recorded as `Action` rows by `ActionRepository::declare()`), `runs/{id}/progress`, `runs/{id}/output`, `runs/{id}/finish` and `credits`. `RunQueue` takes a run in one conditional `UPDATE` (`RunRepository::take()`), refuses news about a run the machine no longer holds (409) and puts back the runs whose lease ended. `RunOutputs` keeps the files under `private/pilot/runs/<run id>/`, each name reduced to a safe one, and `PilotController::output()` shows an image or a video under the type of its extension (`RunOutputs::mediaType()`) and downloads any other file, sandboxed.

## Importing the artifact

```bash
php bin/console c975l:pilot:import <export-folder>
```

The folder holds one sub-folder per collection of the artifact database (`settings`, `tasks`, `docs`), one JSON file per document, the character sheets left aside. `ArtifactReader` reads it into rows, `ArtifactImporter` writes them. A second import updates instead of duplicating: projects and files are matched on their slug, cards on `project:id`, each matched card getting back the artifact's title, note, date, checklist and done state.

## Do not

- Re-import once cards are dated or closed in the back office: the import puts the artifact's values back on every card it matches.
- Put a project colour in a `style` attribute: the Content-Security-Policy refuses it, the colour goes through a `pilot-color-<slug>` class written in the nonced `<style>` of `_layout.html.twig`.
- Pass a machine token in a URL, or serve a run's file inline under a sniffed type: a machine can send an html or svg file, `PilotController::output()` downloads it.
- Check a right in a template only: every action goes through `PilotPageTrait::guard()` (404 for what the account does not see, 403 or a refusal for what its role does not hold), and every list through `PilotScope`.
- Point a setting at EasyAdmin: the settings live on `SettingsController`, the back office only links to `/pilot`.
